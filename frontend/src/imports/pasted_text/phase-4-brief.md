CORE PURPOSE
==================================================

Phase 4 is the CORE DECISION-MAKING EXPERIENCE of EcoTrail.

The user has entered a natural-language travel request.

Now EcoTrail must turn that request into:

STRUCTURED REQUIREMENTS
↓
TRAVEL OPTIONS
↓
RECOMMENDATIONS
↓
ECO-TWIN
↓
GREEN & ACCESSIBLE SCORE
↓
SHOW YOUR MATH
↓
COMPARISON
↓

↓
ITINERARY GENERATION

The experience must make EcoTrail's core innovation obvious.

EcoTrail does not simply generate a travel itinerary.

WHAT SHOULD I CHOOSE?

WHY IS IT BETTER?

WHAT WILL I SAVE?

WHAT WILL I TRADE OFF?

46. IMPORTANT AI BOUNDARY
==================================================

The UI should communicate that AI helps with:

Natural-language understanding
Requirement extraction
Recommendation explanation
User-facing summaries

AI should NOT be visually presented as the authority for:

Carbon calculations
Accessibility verification
Official travel schedules
Official prices
Official booking links

Those come from appropriate data/backend systems.
