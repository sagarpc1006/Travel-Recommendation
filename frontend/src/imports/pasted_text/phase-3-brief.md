51. FINAL DESIGN AUDIT
==================================================

Before finalizing Phase 3, review the entire design.

HOME:

- Is the AI Command Center the primary focus?
- Does Home feel personalized?
- Is the page calm rather than dashboard-heavy?
- Are supporting sections secondary?

AI:

- Are all major AI states designed?
- Does AI feel useful rather than gimmicky?
- Are recommendations understandable?
- Is data provenance visible?

DISCOVER:

- Is search intuitive?
- Are filters useful?
- Are destination cards scannable?
- Does list + map work together?

DESTINATION:

- Is the preview useful without becoming overwhelming?
- Are accessibility and sustainability visible?
- Are evidence levels clear?

RESPONSIVE:

- Does mobile feel intentionally designed?
- Are filters usable?
- Is the AI input comfortable?
- Is the map usable?

ACCESSIBILITY:

- Is important information never color-only?
- Are maps supported by textual alternatives?
- Are focus states visible?

IMPLEMENTATION:

- Can React + Vanilla CSS realistically implement this?
- Are interactions realistic?
- Are components reusable?
